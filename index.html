Research Purpose and Procedures 研究目的与流程

This research investigates methods that generate 3D scene videos from artistic paintings (e.g., traditional Chinese paintings). You will be asked to complete an online questionnaire: you will view several scenes, each consisting of an input painting and six videos generated by six different methods. For each scene, you will rate every video on four criteria (content & composition faithfulness, style faithfulness, spatial plausibility, and overall visual aesthetics) on a 1-5 scale, and then select the video you prefer most. The questionnaire takes approximately 10-15 minutes.

本研究旨在探讨由艺术图像（如中国画）自动生成三维场景视频的相关方法。您将被邀请完成一份在线问卷，对生成的视频进行评价：您将看到若干场景，每个场景包含 1 幅输入原画和 6 段由不同方法生成的视频；请对每段视频按四项指标（内容与构图忠实度、风格忠实度、空间层次可信度、总体视觉美感）打 1–5 分，并选出您最偏好的视频。整个过程约需 10–15 分钟。

Data Collection and Usage 数据收集与使用

We will collect the following data: your background information (gender, age, major, and relevant experience), your ratings and preference choices, and your answers to open-ended questions. The collected data will be used solely for the purpose of this research and will be presented in an anonymized, aggregated form. The data will not be linked to any information that could identify you personally, and will not be disclosed to any third party or used for any other purpose.

我们将收集以下数据：您的背景信息（性别、年龄、专业及相关经验）、您的评分与偏好选择，以及开放性问题的回答。所收集的数据仅用于本研究，并以匿名、汇总的形式呈现；数据不会与任何可识别您个人身份的信息相关联，不会披露给任何第三方，也不会用于本研究以外的任何用途。

Participant Rights 参与者权益

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop and withdraw from this study at any time, and you may skip any question you prefer not to answer. Withdrawal will not result in any penalty or loss of benefits to you. If you have any questions about this study, please feel free to contact the researchers.

您的参与完全自愿。您可以随时中止并退出本研究，也可以跳过任何您不愿回答的题目，退出不会带来任何不利影响。如对本研究有任何疑问，请随时联系研究人员。
